The Kernel Series

The Kernel: A Reading Journal Through the Teachings of Jesus

Coming soon to Amazon

What if the most meaningful way to read the teachings of Jesus was simply to slow down?

The Kernel is a reading journal that gathers the teachings of Jesus from the Synoptic Gospels (Matthew, Mark, and Luke) into a single, thoughtfully organized volume. Rather than rushing from chapter to chapter, this journal invites you to spend time with each teaching, reflect honestly, and record your own journey.

Inside you’ll find:

  • The teachings of Jesus from the Synoptic Gospels using the public-domain World English Bible (WEB)
  • Thoughtfully organized readings that can be explored at your own pace
  • Reflection prompts to encourage personal insight and meaningful conversation
  • Generous space for journaling, questions, and notes

There is no schedule to keep and no finish line to cross. Read a page a day, a few each week, or revisit the journal year after year. Use it alone, with your family, or as part of a small group.

Whether you’re opening the Gospels for the first time or returning after many years, The Kernel offers a simple invitation:

Read slowly.
Reflect honestly.
Keep growing.
