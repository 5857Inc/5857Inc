The Kernel Series

The Kernel: A Journaling Bible Through the Synoptic Gospels

Available now on Amazon

The Kernel is the complete text of the Synoptic Gospels — Matthew, Mark, and Luke — presented in the public-domain World English Bible (WEB) translation, with room to slow down built into every page.

Every page of Scripture is followed by a facing blank page marked “Journal Notes” — space to write, reflect, pray, or simply sit with what you’ve just read. There’s no prompt to follow and no right way to use it. It’s yours.

Inside you’ll find:

  • The complete, unabridged text of Matthew, Mark, and Luke
  • Clean, continuous reading — no commentary, no interruptions
  • A full blank page after every page of Scripture, ready for your own notes

There’s no schedule to keep and no finish line to cross. Read a page a day or a chapter at a time — however you actually spend time with it works.

Whether you’re reading alone, with your family, or leading a small group, The Kernel is built around one idea: giving Scripture — and your own reflection on it — room to breathe.
